- **Thing:** A one-page marketing idea generator that gives business owners a curated set of creative promotional ideas personalized with their company name.
- **Audience:** A new business owner who wants practical and original ways to promote their company but does not know where to begin.
- **Requirements:** Visitors can enter their company name, choose an industry and marketing goal, and generate three tailored marketing idea cards. Each card explains the concept, how to use it, and why it fits the selected goal.
- **Guardrails:** Static browser code. No required external service, keys, accounts, runtime AI, or private data. Generate results from a built-in collection of curated ideas. Label fictional or sample content. Preserve the example and publishing setup. Work on a branch and wait for human review before shipping.
- **Experience:** Professional yet interactive, with a polished business-focused layout, clear form controls, engaging idea cards, and an obvious visual relationship between the visitor’s inputs and personalized results.
